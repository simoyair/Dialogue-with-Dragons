Friday Nights at The Hearth in Osaka

Every Friday, we play a story game in English at The Hearth in Osaka. You make a character, and you tell a story together with new friends. You don't need any experience.

Playing with us doesn't cost anything. The Hearth does ask for a small fee to play games there: 700 yen, and then you can play as long as you like until close. They also have coffee, tea, and other drinks if you'd like something.

We play the same fun adventure every week, so you can join any Friday that works for you.
